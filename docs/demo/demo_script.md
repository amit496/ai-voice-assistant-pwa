Demo Script & Recording Checklist

Goal: Record a 30-second demo that shows a complete speech-to-speech interaction for portfolio/recruiter.

Steps to record:
1. Start the app locally or open the deployed demo URL.
2. Open devtools Console > Console tab to show logs.
3. Click the microphone button.
4. Speak a simple prompt: "What's the weather like today in Mumbai?"
5. Wait for the assistant to respond with voice.
6. Show console logs: `🎙️ [AGENT SESSION]`, `📡 [VOICE]`, `✅ [VOICE]` etc.
7. If fallback happens, show `/api/chat` call and the GROQ reply.
8. End recording and save as `demo-30s.mp4`.

Notes for camera capture:
- Include a small inset showing your face (optional)
- Zoom into the app briefly while audio plays
- Keep the clip under 45 seconds for social posts

Suggested caption for video post:
"Live demo: Real-time speech-to-speech AI conversation built with ElevenLabs + GROQ. Full project in the repo — link in bio."
